Slot Watcher: Android App Requirements Specification

Version 1.0 · 18 Nov 2026 · Author: Md. Nur A Alam

1. Purpose

Slot Watcher is a personal Android app that logs in to the Mojaru Teacher Panel at times the examiner chooses and checks whether any pre-assessment slot is booked. If any are, it rings a spoken alarm such as “2 slots booked: 6:30 PM, 7:10 PM”.

Today the examiner opens Chrome, logs in and opens the Pre Assessment List by hand every few minutes during the evening. The app replaces those repeated manual checks.

2. Scope and assumptions

One user: the examiner, on their own Android phone, using their own Mojaru teacher account.

In scope

  • Saving the Mojaru login (email/mobile + password) on the device
  • Setting up a watch session: date, start time, end time, gap
  • Checking the Pre Assessment List automatically at each scheduled time
  • A spoken alarm listing how many slots are booked and their times
  • A history of what each check found

Out of scope

  • Booking, cancelling or editing slots
  • Joining sessions, marking present/absent, or anything else in the panel
  • iOS, web or desktop versions
  • Use by other examiners or with more than one account

Assumptions

  • The panel at teacher.mojaru.com stays reachable and its pages keep their current layout.
  • The Pre Assessment List can show any chosen date. Its date box defaults to today.
  • A slot counts as booked when its Slot Status is not “available” (for example “booked” or “upcoming”). This needs checking against a real booked slot.

3. Current manual workflow

Each manual check takes 7 steps, and the examiner repeats them every 10 minutes or so through the evening.

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Go to teacher.mojaru.com/teacher/login.
  3. Sign in on the “Sign in to Mojaru Teacher Panel” form with Email/Mobile and Password.
  4. On the Dashboard (/teacher/dashboard), open the side menu.
  5. Choose Pre Assessment Program → Pre Assessment List.
  6. Check the date box in “At a Glance” (it shows today, e.g. 18-11-2026) and read the Booked Slots count.
  7. If it is above 0, scroll the Pre-Assessments Program table to find which rows are booked.

4. Functional requirements

The user enters a date, start time, end time and gap once. The app then checks at every gap step from start to end, and rings each time it finds booked slots.

Credentials

IDRequirement
FR-1The user can enter and save a Mojaru Email/Mobile and Password.
FR-2The app checks the login at save time and shows “Login OK” or the panel’s error message.
FR-3The user can edit or delete the saved login.

Watch session

IDRequirement
FR-4The user enters a date (date picker), a start time, an end time and a gap in minutes.
FR-5The app lists the check times before starting. Example: 18-11-2026, 6:25 PM to 9:15 PM, gap 10 → 6:25, 6:35, 6:45 … 9:05, 9:15 PM (18 checks).
FR-6If the end time is not on a gap step, the last check is the last step before it.
FR-7Start time must be before end time, gap must be at least 1 minute, and the date can’t be in the past.
FR-8Start sets the session going. Stop cancels all remaining checks.
FR-9One session runs at a time. Starting a new one replaces the old one after the user confirms.
FR-10If the session is started after its start time, checks skip the missed steps and resume at the next gap step.

Each check

IDRequirement
FR-11At each check time the app logs in (or reuses a valid session) and opens the Pre Assessment List for the chosen date.
FR-12It reads the Booked Slots count and the table rows, and records each booked slot’s time range (e.g. 18:30 - 18:40).
FR-13A slot counts as booked when its Slot Status is anything other than “available” or “expired”.
FR-14If the table spans several pages, the app reads all of them.
FR-15Each check is logged with its time, booked count, booked slot times and result (OK / error).

Alarm

IDRequirement
FR-16If one or more slots are booked, the app rings an alarm on every check while any slot is booked.
FR-17The alarm plays a sound and speaks the result, for example: “2 slots booked: 6:30 PM and 7:10 PM.”
FR-18The alarm shows full-screen, even over the lock screen, with the count and the slot times.
FR-19The alarm keeps ringing and repeats the spoken message until the user taps Dismiss.
FR-20Dismissing the alarm doesn’t stop the session. The next check still runs.
FR-21If no slot is booked, the app stays silent and only updates the status notification (“6:35 PM — 0 booked”).

Errors

IDRequirement
FR-22No internet or the site doesn’t respond: retry up to 3 times, 30 seconds apart, then notify “Check at 6:35 PM failed”.
FR-23Login rejected (e.g. password changed): ring a distinct alarm saying “Login failed” and pause the session.
FR-24Page layout not recognised: notify “Couldn’t read slots” so the user can check by hand.

History

IDRequirement
FR-25A History screen lists past checks, newest first, with booked count and slot times.

5. Non-functional requirements

The hardest requirement is reliability: every check must run on time, even with the screen off, the app closed or battery saver on.

IDAreaRequirement
NFR-1TimingEach check starts within 1 minute of its scheduled time, with the screen off and the app in the background.
NFR-2BackgroundChecks run from exact alarms (AlarmManager) plus a short foreground task, so Doze and battery saver can’t delay them. The app asks for “Alarms & reminders” and battery-optimisation exemption at setup.
NFR-3RebootA session that’s still running comes back after the phone restarts.
NFR-4Alarm audibilityThe alarm uses the alarm audio channel, so it rings even when the phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb.
NFR-5SecurityThe password is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore. It never leaves the phone except to teacher.mojaru.com over HTTPS.
NFR-6PrivacyNo analytics, ads or third-party servers. Participant names and mobile numbers aren’t stored.
NFR-7Load on MojaruAt most one login and one list request per check. The minimum gap is 1 minute.
NFR-8BatteryA 3-hour session with a 10-minute gap uses under 3% of the battery.
NFR-9PlatformAndroid 8.0 (API 26) and later. Tested on the examiner’s phone and Android 14+.
NFR-10LanguageScreens in English. The spoken alarm in English, with Bangla as an option (see Open questions).

7. Technical approach

Mojaru has no public API, so the app has to read the same pages the browser does. The first development task is to choose between the two ways of doing that.

OptionHow it worksStrengthsWeaknesses
A. Direct HTTP (preferred)Capture the login and slot-list requests the panel makes (Chrome DevTools → Network), then call them from the app with OkHttp/Retrofit and parse the responseFast, light on battery, reliable in the backgroundNeeds investigation first. Breaks if Mojaru changes its API
B. Hidden WebViewLoad the pages in an invisible WebView, fill the login form, read the slot table with JavaScriptWorks without knowing the APIHeavier and less reliable with the screen off. Breaks if the page layout changes

Proposed stack: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose UI, AlarmManager setExactAndAllowWhileIdle to wake the app for each check, a short foreground service to run the check, Android TextToSpeech for the voice, Room for history, and DataStore with the Android Keystore for the login.

Whatever the result, the next check is scheduled, so one failure or one dismissed alarm never ends the session.

The app logs in to Mojaru automatically. Before relying on it, confirm with Mojaru that this kind of automated checking of your own account is allowed.

8. Acceptance criteria

The app is done when all of these pass on the examiner’s phone.

  • Date 18-11-2026, 6:25 PM to 9:15 PM, gap 10 shows 18 check times from 6:25 to 9:15 PM, and checks run at those times within 1 minute.
  • With 0 booked, no alarm rings, and the notification shows “0 booked” after each check.
  • When 2 slots are booked (e.g. 18:30 and 19:10), the alarm rings and says “2 slots booked: 6:30 PM and 7:10 PM”, with the screen locked and the phone on silent.
  • The alarm keeps ringing until Dismiss is tapped. It rings again at the next check while the slots are still booked.
  • Checks continue on time with the screen off for the whole 3-hour session, and after a phone restart.
  • A wrong password triggers the “Login failed” alarm and pauses the session.
  • With mobile data and Wi-Fi off, a check retries and then shows “Check failed” without crashing.
  • The password can’t be read from the app’s storage files.
  • Stop cancels all remaining checks.

9. Open questions

  • What does a booked slot’s Slot Status say (“booked”, “upcoming”, something else)? A screenshot of a booked row would settle this.
  • Should the voice speak English, Bangla, or both?
  • Should the alarm also show the participant’s name for each booked slot, or only the time?
  • Does the Pre Assessment List show other dates when the date box is changed, or only today?
  • Does the panel log you out after a while, or allow more than one login at a time (phone app and Chrome together)?
  • Has Mojaru confirmed that automated checking of your own account is allowed?
